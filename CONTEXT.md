# Domain language

**Evidence fact** — A tenant-bound record of a collected setting, relationship, event, or collection boundary. It describes the supplied evidence, not the current state of Microsoft Entra.

**Evidence proof** — A reproducible derivation of a conclusion under versioned rules and recorded assumptions. Its states are supported, refuted, unknown, and conflicting; none certifies successful authentication or effective service authorization.

**Configured grant** — A recorded permission assignment or consent. Avoid “used permission” unless independently supported by activity evidence.

**Control path** — A sequence of supported ownership, federation, identity-instantiation, and application-grant steps that models a possible capability under explicit assumptions. Arbitrary graph reachability is not a control path.

**Collection boundary** — The specific data collection whose completeness determines whether missing records can support a negative conclusion.

**Access contract** — Tenant-bound declarative intent evaluated against configured grants or modeled control paths. A pass requires complete relevant evidence; missing prerequisites produce unknown.

**Change plan** — A proposed set of locally modeled relationship exclusions with declared costs and protected integrations. It never applies a tenant change.

**Temporal witness** — A collected instant or intersection of explicit validity intervals supporting coexistence of prerequisites. Repeated observations alone do not establish continuity between scans.

**Deployment assumption** — An operator-supplied claim about when a workload can use credential metadata. Metadata validity does not establish possession, deployment, or observed use.

**Investigation package** — A bounded, versioned projection of facts, query, and proof for offline replay. Integrity hashes detect modification, not Microsoft provenance; pseudonyms do not anonymize topology.
